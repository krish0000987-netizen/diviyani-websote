Frequently Asked Questions

Clarifying Our Engagement Model

How do we receive our project quotation?

After you submit your project brief via our Start a Project page or WhatsApp, our creative director reviews your exact scope and provides a detailed formal proposal within 4-12 hours.

Do you work with businesses outside Patna?

Yes. While our creative studio is headquartered in Khadamkua, Patna, we regularly collaborate with brands across Bihar, Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, and international clients via structured digital workflows.

Who owns the final master design assets?

You do. Upon project completion and final signoff, 100% full intellectual property and commercial copyright of all finalized vector and raster assets transfer to your company.
