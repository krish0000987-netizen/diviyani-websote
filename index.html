Brand Identity

Aurora Haute Parfumerie

Client

Luxury Fragrance House

Timeline / Year

2026

Key Scope

Monogram, Packaging, Art Direction

The Challenge

Establishing an ultra-luxury sensory brand that stands shoulder-to-shoulder with European fragrance maisons while carrying distinctive artisanal warmth.

Creative Direction

Dark obsidian backgrounds with bespoke high-contrast serif typography, precision gold foil accents, and tactile embossed paper stocks.

The Solution

A bespoke identity system featuring a refined interlocking monogram, custom structural packaging boxes, and a cinematic visual campaign for digital and flagship launch.

Outcome & Assets

Complete brand design system delivered with 40+ master asset files, packaging production dies, and brand guideline documentation.
